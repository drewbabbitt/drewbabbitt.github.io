Services

Non-surgical hair replacement

No surgery, no downtime. Every solution is chosen and fitted in the studio, then maintained on a schedule that suits you.

01

Hair Systems

A hair system is a custom-fitted piece attached to the scalp, matched to your hair colour, density and growth pattern, then cut and styled in the studio so the edges disappear. Nothing is surgical and there is no recovery period.

Most often chosen for male pattern loss and advanced thinning.

02

Toppers & Wigs

Toppers add density where you have lost it, usually at the crown and along the part, while keeping your own hair around the sides. Full wigs cover complete loss. Both come in human hair and synthetic, in a range of caps and attachment methods.

Most often chosen by women with diffuse thinning, alopecia, or hair loss from treatment.

03

Cut, Color & Styling

A system only looks right once it is cut into your face and blended with the hair you still have. Styling and colour matching happen in the same appointment as the fitting, and you leave able to do it yourself.

Included with a new fitting; also available on its own.

04

Maintenance

Systems need cleaning, re-attaching and periodic adjustment. Maintenance visits keep the fit secure and the hair in condition, and are the difference between a system that looks new and one that does not.

Every four to six weeks for most clients.
